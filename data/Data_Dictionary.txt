DATA DICTIONARY / CODEBOOK
========================================================================
Project: Language Education in a Brave New World
Data File Reference: brave world Research_Data_Complete.xlsx
Prepared by: Pegah Marikh

This document defines the key variables and metrics analyzed in the 
study across the quantitative and qualitative phases.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. LINGUISTIC FEATURES (Sheet: Linguistic_Features)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Participant_ID: Unique identifier for participants (EFL-01 to EFL-60).
- Mode: Writing condition.
  * "Raw": Independent, unassisted student writing.
  * "Revised": Text edited/polished by GPT-4o using a standard prompt.
- Lexical_Diversity: Measured via MTLD (Measure of Textual Lexical Diversity).
- Syntactic_Complexity: Mean Length of Clause (MLC) and Mean Length of 
  T-unit (MLT).
- Error_Rate: Grammar, spelling, and mechanical errors per 100 words.
- Stance_Markers: Frequency of self-mention markers (e.g., "I", "my", "we") 
  and hedges/boosters per 100 words.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. AUTHENTICITY GAP SCALE (AGS) (Sheets: AGS_Items & AGS_Subscales)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The AGS measures students' psychological and identity shifts post-AI revision 
across three subscales (5-point Likert Scale: 1 = Strongly Disagree, 
5 = Strongly Agree):

- Identity Compression (IC): The feeling that one's personal voice, 
  cultural nuances, and unique style are flattened or standardized by AI.
- Authenticity Gap (AG): The discrepancy between the student's actual 
  linguistic capability and the polished output presented as their work.
- Reduced Linguistic Agency (RLA): The perceived loss of control and ownership 
  over the text production and revision process.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THEMATIC CODING (Sheet: Interview_Codes)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Qualitative codes from the semi-structured interviews with 15 participants:
- Code_LossOfVoice: Statements indicating a sense of losing personal voice.
- Code_DeceptivePolishing: Concerns regarding the ethical dilemma of 
  submitting work that feels "too perfect."
- Code_OverReliance: Dependence on AI for cognitive and linguistic drafting.
- Code_AgenticReclamation: Active strategies used by students to push back 
  against AI standardization.
