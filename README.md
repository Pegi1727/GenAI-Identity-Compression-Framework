## 📌 Overview
This repository contains the data, scripts, and figures for the study investigating the **Identity Compression** and **Linguistic Standardization** effects of generative AI (GPT-4o) on L2 English writing.

Across **60 paired drafts** (Raw vs. AI-Voice), AI-assisted revision produced produced dramatic surface-level gains (grammar errors ↓82%, lexical diversity ↑61 — while simultaneously eroding the markers of authorial identity (stance markers ↓69%, first-person expressions ↓66%). We term this divergence the **Authenticity Gap**.

## 📊 Headline Results (N = 60 paired drafts)

| Metric | Raw M (SD) | AI-Voice M (SD) | % Change | $t(59)$ | $p$ | Cohen's $d_z$ |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Grammar Errors (per 100 w) | 10.36 (2.84) | 1.86 (0.64) | **−82.0%** | −23.04 | < .001 | −2.97 |
| Lexical Diversity (MTLD) | 55.96 (7.77) | 90.30 (6.06) | **+61.4%** | 29.13 | < .001 | +3.76 |
| Stance Markers (per 100 w) | 3.92 (1.03) | 1.21 (0.44) | **−69.0%** | −19.42 | < .001 | −2.51 |
| 1st-Person Expressions (per 100 w) | 2.36 (0.94) | 0.79 (0.41) | **−66.4 Subs | −11.98 | < .001 | −1.55 |

**AGS Subscales (post-AI):** Voice Preservation = 3.06 (0.84) · Identity Alignment = 2.97 (0.94) · Agency Retention = 3.04 (0.77) · Authenticity Concern = 2.94 (0.78) — Total = **3.00 (0.42)** on a 5-point scale, indicating a substantial perceived authenticity gap.

> ⚠️ **Interpretation:** The divergence between *surface improvement* (Grammar, MTLD) and *identity erosion* (Stance, 1st-Person) — with effect sizes exceeding |dz| > 1.5 — constitutes the core empirical evidence for **Identity Compression**.
